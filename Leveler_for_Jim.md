# Leveler — LEGO build plan

From *FernGully: The Last Rainforest* (1992). Watch it first.

**Build at 32 studs long** (~26 cm). Not 24 — the tracks won't fit at 24.

---

## Part 1: the body (bricks)

Use `Leveler_32studs_parts.csv` — 490 pieces.

| Colour | Qty |
|---|---|
| Dark Bluish Gray | 186 |
| Black | 160 |
| Dark Orange | 99 |
| Light Bluish Gray | 37 |

Mostly 1x1, 1x2, 1x3 and 1x4 bricks. Build from the tub first, wrong
colours and all. Only the orange really matters for it looking right.

**Skip the black 1x1s that make the tread pattern.** Real tracks are going
there instead.

---

## Part 2: the Technic (the bits that move)

### Tracks that roll

| Part | No. | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| Technic Link Tread | 3873 | 72 |
| Technic Gear 24 Tooth | 3648 | 4 |
| Technic Axle 4L | 3705 | 2 |
| Technic Bush | 3713 | 4 |
| Technic Brick 1 x 4 | 3701 | 4 |

Two 24-tooth gears per side act as sprockets, ~36 links per loop. The
Technic bricks go inside the hull to carry the axles.

### Boom that raises and lowers

| Part | No. | Qty |
|---|---|---|

Friction pins hold the boom at whatever angle you set it. No motor needed.

| Part | No. | Qty |

The 24-tooth gear **is** the saw blade. The gear teeth look exactly like
saw teeth. Nobody has to explain that one.

## Part 3: the bit that's actually the point

Bricklink Studio is free. Let the boy drive it.

1. He opens `Leveler_LEGO.obj` himself.
2. He drags the size slider. The model rebuilds live, coarse to fine.
3. He clicks **Instructions**. Studio generates a step-by-step booklet in
   thirty seconds — the same kind that comes in the box.

Step 3 is the moment. He has held fifty of those booklets and assumed a
person drew every page.

If he bites: open the .obj in Notepad. It's a text file. It's just
numbers. Every 3D model in every game he plays is that.
